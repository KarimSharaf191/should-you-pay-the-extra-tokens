STUDY 1 - EVIDENCE ABLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Does removing the gold evidence change the prediction?
  random_ctrl removes an equal amount of NON-evidence text, so a
  drop under 'ablated' beyond 'random_ctrl' is evidence-specific.

  n=89 instances with gold evidence (89 matched to a full-context baseline)

  variant           accuracy   flip vs full
  full(baseline)       0.753            nan
  ablated              0.393          0.393
  random_ctrl          0.753          0.034
  oracle               0.831          0.191

  Evidence-specific effect = acc(random_ctrl) - acc(ablated) = +0.360
  Near zero means the model was not relying on the gold evidence:
  removing it cost no more than removing arbitrary text of the same size.

STUDY 3 - AGENTIC STOPPING FAITHFULNESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  When does gold evidence arrive, and does the model answer before it does?

  n=150 trajectories (89 with gold evidence)
  mean answer step            : 2.57
  mean unique spans seen      : 7.5
  gold recall at stop         : 0.570
  ANSWERED WITHOUT ANY GOLD   : 19/89 = 21.3%
    ...of those, 9/19 were still scored CORRECT (47.4%) - right answer, no evidence
  mean step gold first appears: 0.23
  turns where the model emitted neither SEARCH nor ANSWER (fell back to hypothesis as query): 1

STUDY 4 - CITATION FAITHFULNESS IN CoT RATIONALES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Are quoted passages real, and does the rationale track the gold evidence?

  n=150  |  rationales containing quotes: 41
  quote groundedness (quoted text found verbatim in contract): 0.760
  content-word overlap with GOLD evidence   : 0.554
  content-word overlap with control spans   : 0.211
  lift over chance                          : +0.343
  A lift near zero means the rationale is no more anchored to the
  operative clause than to arbitrary contract text.
